How to read this

          
    
            
  • 
              Tokens/word divides every emitted ID, without
              special tokens, by the number of whitespace-separated words.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Lossless means
              decode(encode(text)) === text after NFC and
              whitespace normalization. "no" means the round trip changed the
              text.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Encode is the median of three runs, timed one
              tokenizer at a time after every tokenizer has loaded and been
              warmed up once, so no run overlaps another tokenizer's work.
              Absolute numbers depend on your device.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              The banglatok models are trained on the
              banglatok corpus;
              the other tokenizers never saw that training split.
            
    • 
          

        

      


      

        

          Tokenizer files are served from this Space and loaded with
          transformers.js.
          tiktoken encodings use
          gpt-tokenizer.
          Nothing you paste leaves your browser.
